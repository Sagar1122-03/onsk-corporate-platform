ONSK OCR Software

"Turn Handwritten and Printed Documents Into Digital Data Instantly."

Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)

ONSK OCR is an intelligent document processing solution designed to extract information from handwritten and printed documents and convert it into structured digital text. It helps businesses reduce manual data entry, improve productivity, and simplify document management.

Core OCR Capabilities

1. Handwritten Recognition

Parses cursive, varied stroke weights, and freeform handwriting on notes and applications.

2. Printed Text Extraction

High-speed extraction from machine-printed receipts, forms, invoices, and standard documents.

3. Key-Value Extraction

Intelligently categorizes key-value pairs such as payee names, amounts, account numbers, and dates.

4. Multi-Format Ingestion

Processes diverse raster files including PNG, JPG, and multi-page TIFF images.
