Core OCR Capabilities

1. Handwritten Recognition

Parses cursive, varied stroke weights, and freeform handwriting on notes and applications.

2. Printed Text Extraction

High-speed extraction from machine-printed receipts, forms, invoices, and standard documents.

3. Key-Value Extraction

Intelligently categorizes key-value pairs such as payee names, amounts, account numbers, and dates.

4. Multi-Format Ingestion
